# Desert City-States — One-Page Design Doc

**Genre:** Small-scale 4X strategy  
**Theme:** Rival oasis city-states in a harsh desert  
**Platform:** Browser/PC, turn-based, single-screen friendly

## High Concept

Players lead a desert city-state competing for survival and dominance across a sparse hex map. The core tension is water scarcity and trade-route control: expand to oases, connect them with caravans, defend them from raiders, and outmaneuver rival city-states.

## Design Goals

- Simple enough to vibe-code fast
- Distinct theme without being weird
- Strategic depth from scarcity and route control
- Clear, readable map and short turns

## Core Loop

1. Scout nearby hexes
2. Found or upgrade a city on an oasis
3. Build caravan routes between cities
4. Earn Water, Wealth, and Influence
5. Spend resources on expansion, defense, and upgrades
6. Fight over routes and oases
7. Win by control or score

## Map & Tiles

- **Oasis:** city site, high value
- **Dunes:** slow movement, low value
- **Salt Flats:** fast movement, poor value
- **Ridges:** defensive chokepoints
- **Ruins:** bonus sites or relics

## Resources

- **Water:** growth and city maintenance
- **Wealth:** buildings, units, trade
- **Influence:** diplomacy, special actions

## Cities

Each city can:

- Produce basic resources
- Build improvements
- Train units
- Establish caravan links
- Upgrade into a specialty

Possible city specializations:

- Trade Hub
- Well Fort
- Fortress
- Scholar Outpost

## Units

- **Scout:** reveals map
- **Caravan Guard:** protects trade routes
- **Raider:** attacks routes and weak targets

## Combat

- Automatic resolution
- Terrain modifiers matter
- Strongly favor positioning over unit spam

## Unique Mechanic

**Caravan routes are the real lifeblood of the empire.**  
Controlling roads between oases matters as much as holding the oases themselves.

## Win Conditions

- Control most oases
- Reach a wealth or prestige score
- Hold key relic sites for a set number of turns

## MVP Scope

- Small hex map
- Fog of war
- 3 resources
- 3 unit types
- 1 generic city with simple upgrades
- Caravan route system
- Basic AI expansion + raiding
- 1 victory condition

## What Makes It Fun

- Every oasis matters
- Expansion creates vulnerability
- Trade routes create real strategic choices
- Easy to understand, hard to optimize
